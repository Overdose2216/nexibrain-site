Privacy Policy – DailyMotivationBoost

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the DailyMotivationBoost application handles your information when you use our services.

1. Data Collection

DailyMotivationBoost does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

We do not request personal information such as name, email, phone number, or identifiers.
No data is transmitted to our servers.

2. Use of the Application

DailyMotivationBoost provides:

  • a daily motivational quote,
  • a notification when the quote of the day becomes available (starting at 8:00 AM),
  • advertisements that help support the development of the application.

All app features work without requiring any personal user data.

3. Notifications

DailyMotivationBoost sends one daily notification to inform you that the new motivational quote is available.

  • Notifications are generated locally on your device.
  • No personal data is collected related to notifications.
  • You may disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

4. Advertising (AdMob)

DailyMotivationBoost displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

To comply with privacy laws such as GDPR (Europe), the app includes a consent request.
Depending on your region, you may be asked to choose between:

  • personalized ads,
  • non-personalized ads,
  • or refusing ad personalization.

AdMob may collect certain data directly, such as:

  • advertising identifiers (e.g. AAID/IDFA),
  • interaction with ads (impressions, clicks),
  • device information (type, model, OS),
  • IP address (used for approximate geolocation).

These data are handled by Google, not by DailyMotivationBoost.

Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage ad personalization anytime through your device's Google settings.

5. Third-Party Data Processing

DailyMotivationBoost does not share any personal data because none is collected by us.
Any data processed comes directly from interactions with Google AdMob according to their own policies.

6. Security

Although DailyMotivationBoost does not store personal information, we are committed to providing a secure and reliable experience for all users.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if we introduce new features, modify advertising partners, or adjust the app's behavior due to legal requirements.

The latest version will always be available within the app and/or on our website.

8. Contact
