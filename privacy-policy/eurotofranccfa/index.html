Privacy Policy – EuroToFrancCFA

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the EuroToFrancCFA application handles your data when you use it.

1. Data Collection

The EuroToFrancCFA app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

We do not request access to your device, no login is required, and no personal information is ever gathered.

2. Use of the Application

The app only performs the following functions:

  • converting Euros (EUR) to West African CFA francs (XOF),
  • converting West African CFA francs (XOF) to Euros (EUR).

No user data is recorded or analyzed.

3. Advertising (AdMob)

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect certain data for ad personalization and measurement purposes, including but not limited to:

  • advertising identifiers,
  • ad interaction data,
  • device information and technical details.

These data are collected directly by Google, which acts as a third-party provider.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage your ad personalization preferences through your device's Google settings.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not share any personal data, as we do not collect any.

The only data potentially processed by a third party are those collected directly by AdMob for advertising purposes.

5. Security

Even though no data is collected through the app, we remain committed to ensuring a safe and compliant user experience.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be displayed within this section of the application or on the Play Store listing.

We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
