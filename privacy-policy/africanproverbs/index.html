Privacy Policy – AfricanProverbs

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data our mobile application collects, how it is used, and what rights you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Personal Data

Our application does not collect any personally identifiable information.
This means:

  • No name
  • No email address
  • No phone number
  • No user profile data

1.2. Anonymous Authentication

To send daily notifications, we use an anonymous identifier generated by the notification service (Firebase Cloud Messaging or equivalent).

This identifier:

  • cannot be used to identify you personally
  • is used solely for delivering notifications
  • is not shared with any third party except the notification provider

1.3. Usage Data

The app itself does not record or store any personal usage data.

2. Advertising – Google AdMob

Our application uses Google AdMob to display ads.
Google may collect anonymous information through its SDKs, such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., AAID, IDFA)
  • Device information
  • App usage data
  • Approximate location (if enabled by the user)

This information is used only to:

  • provide ads
  • improve ad relevance
  • prevent advertising fraud

Google handles this data according to its own Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The app itself does not use cookies.
However, advertising partners (Google AdMob) may use similar technologies to personalize ads.

Users can:

  • disable ad personalization in their device settings
  • reset their advertising ID at any time

4. How We Use the Information

The collected data is used solely for:

  • Sending daily notifications through an anonymous identifier
  • Delivering AdMob advertisements
  • Improving the user experience (anonymously)

We do not sell any data and we do not share any information with third parties other than the essential service providers (Google/Firebase).

5. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the anonymous data we handle.
No sensitive personal information is stored.

6. User Rights

Users can:

  • Disable notifications from their device settings
  • Disable Google ad personalization
  • Uninstall the application at any time, which removes all local data

7. Third-Party Services Used

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (anonymous push notifications)

These services may collect anonymous data according to their own privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time.
Any changes will be reflected within the app or on the dedicated page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
Email: contact@nexibrain.com
