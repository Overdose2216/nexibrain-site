Privacy Policy – GoalVision (previously MatchVision / FootGoal)

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the FootGoal application handles your data when you use our services.

1. Data Collection

Currently, FootGoal does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

We do not request access to your device, and no personal information (such as name, email, location, or identifiers) is collected or saved.

2. Use of the Application

FootGoal provides the following features:

  • live football scores and match results,
  • predictions generated from internal statistics and analysis,
  • display of advertisements to support app development.

None of these features require or use any personal user data.

3. Advertising (AdMob)

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect certain data for ad personalization and analytics, including but not limited to:

  • advertising identifiers,
  • ad interaction data,
  • device information and technical details.

These data are collected directly by Google, acting as a third-party provider.

For more details, you can review Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage your ad personalization settings from your device's Google account settings.

4. Subscriptions (Future Feature)

In a future update, FootGoal will offer a paid subscription system, which may include:

  • removal of ads,
  • access to premium features.

When this system is implemented, certain limited information may be handled automatically by Google Play Billing, such as:

  • subscription status,
  • purchase history,
  • Google Play–generated identifiers.

FootGoal will not have access to your personal payment information.

Any update involving the collection of personal data will be clearly stated in this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

The application does not share any personal data, as none is collected.

The only data potentially processed are those collected directly by Google services (AdMob or Google Play Billing), in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Security

Even though FootGoal does not collect user data, we remain committed to providing a safe and compliant experience.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated, especially when:

  • new features are added,
  • the subscription system is introduced,
  • advertising services are modified.

The latest version will always be available within the app and on our website.
