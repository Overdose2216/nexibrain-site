Privacy Policy GDPR

Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how Nexibrain Technologies ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when using our mobile applications. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is protected.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Data: Information you voluntarily provide when you register or interact with our Apps, such as your name, email address, and other contact details.
  • Device ID: We collect your device ID for the purposes of serving ads and providing game services.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our Apps, including your interactions with the content and features within the Apps.
  • Location Data (IP Address): We collect your IP address to automatically determine your country. This allows us to provide localized content, such as identifying the correct TV broadcasters for football matches in your region.

When you sign in with Google, we collect your Google User ID, email address, and name to create your account and manage your subscription.

This applies to all our applications:

  • GoalVision: AI Soccer Tips (previously published as MatchVision: AI Soccer Live & Predictions)
  • Aura: Daily Affirmations (previously published as Daily Affirmations & Quotes)
  • Euro to CFA Converter

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Improve Our Services: We use your information to operate, maintain, and enhance our Apps.
  • Advertising: Your device ID is used to serve targeted ads through third-party ad networks like AdMob.
  • Game Services: Your device ID is used to enable features such as leaderboards, achievements, and other game-related services.
  • Localization: We use your IP address to display relevant sports broadcasting information (TV channels) and local match times based on your geographical area.
  • Subscription Management: We use your email and unique identifiers provided by RevenueCat to manage your Premium Access and ensure your purchases are available across all your devices.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. However, we may share your data with third-party service providers who assist us in delivering our services, such as ad networks and game service providers.

We use RevenueCat to process and manage in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat receives anonymized transaction data and your User ID to validate your Premium status.

We may share your anonymized IP address with third-party geolocation services (such as IPAPI or similar providers) solely to determine your country of residence for content localization.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide you with our services and to comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access: You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You have the right to correct any inaccuracies in your personal data.
  • Erasure: You have the right to request that we delete your personal data. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data (including your Firestore profile and Firebase authentication) directly within the App settings or contacting us at contact@nexibrain.com. Your data will be deleted within 48 hours upon request (mail, name, picture, uid…)
  • Objection: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Revocation of Consent: If you have given consent for the processing of your personal data, you have the right to revoke that consent at any time.

6. Revocation of Consent

If you wish to revoke your consent to the processing of your data, you can do so by contacting us at contact@nexibrain.com

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and we encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@nexibrain.com
