FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Is GoalVision a betting app?

No. GoalVision offers no real-money betting and is not linked to any bookmaker. Predictions are provided for information and entertainment, to enrich your experience as a football fan.

Are the predictions guaranteed?

No prediction can be guaranteed — and nobody should promise you otherwise. That's exactly why GoalVision publishes its real 7-day track record: you judge the AI's reliability on facts, not slogans.

Is the app free?

Yes, GoalVision is free to download and the essentials are free to use. A Premium subscription (with a 3-day free trial) unlocks all predictions; some content can also be unlocked match by match by watching an ad.

Which leagues are covered?

The top European leagues (Ligue 1, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga…), the European cups and the 2026 World Cup, with standings, fixtures and statistics.

Which languages is the app available in?

GoalVision is translated into 19 languages, including English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic and Vietnamese. Predictions themselves are generated in your language.
